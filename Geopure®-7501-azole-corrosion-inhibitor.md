# Geopure®-7501: Azole Corrosion Inhibitor (Yellow Metal Protection)

## Product Overview

Tolyltriazole (TTA) based yellow metal corrosion inhibitor for copper, brass, and cupronickel alloys in cooling systems. Forms a chemisorbed protective film (Cu-TTA complex) on copper surfaces. Essential for systems with copper condenser tubes, brass valves, or cupronickel heat exchangers.

## Chemical Composition

| Component | CAS Number | % w/w | Functional Role |
|-----------|-----------|-------|-----------------|
| Tolyltriazole (TTA) | 29385-43-1 | 40.0-50.0 | Copper corrosion inhibitor, forms Cu-N bond |
| Sodium hydroxide (50%) | 1310-73-2 | 8.0-12.0 | Solubilizer (sodium tolytriazolate formation) |
| Demineralized water | 7732-18-5 | Balance | Solvent |

Alternative: Benzotriazole (BTA, CAS 95-14-7) can substitute TTA but has lower chlorine stability and solubility.

### Film Formation Chemistry
TTA forms a polymeric Cu(I)-TTA complex on the copper surface:
Cu + TTA → Cu(I)-TTA (insoluble, protective film)

Film is stable at pH 7-9 and tolerant of moderate oxidant levels. However, continuous high free chlorine (>1 mg/L) or low pH (<6.5) can degrade the film.

## Manufacturing Procedure

### Batch Size: 1000 kg

| Step | Action | Duration | Notes |
|------|--------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Charge 500 kg DM water | - | Warm to 35°C if TTA dissolution is slow |
| 2 | Start agitator at 120 RPM | - | - |
| 3 | Add 100 kg NaOH (50% solution) | 10 min | Exothermic |
| 4 | Slowly add 450 kg TTA (powder or flake) | 30-45 min | Dissolves as sodium salt. May need warming to 40°C |
| 5 | Mix until fully dissolved | 15-20 min | Solution should be clear dark amber |
| 6 | Cool to ambient if needed | - | - |
| 7 | QC sample | - | - |

## Dosage Guidelines

### Target: 2-5 mg/L TTA in recirculating water

```
Product dose in makeup = Target TTA in recirc / (CoC × TTA fraction in product)

Example: Target 3 mg/L TTA, CoC = 5, TTA fraction = 0.45
Dose = 3 / (5 × 0.45) = 1.3 mg/L product in makeup
```

### Typical Dosing

| System | Product Dose in Makeup (mg/L) |
|--------|------------------------------|
| Systems with copper condensers | 1-3 |
| Systems with brass components | 0.5-1.5 |
| No copper alloys present | Not required |

## Storage and Handling

- HDPE or SS316, 5-45°C
- TTA may crystallize below 5°C. Warm and agitate to re-dissolve
- Shelf life: 24 months

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Copper corrosion (blue/green deposits) | Low TTA residual, oxidant attack | Increase TTA dose, reduce free chlorine to <0.5 mg/L |
| TTA consumption rapid | High oxidant dose degrading TTA | Use bromine instead of chlorine (less aggressive to TTA), reduce oxidant dose |
| Brown staining | TTA-iron complex | Check iron levels, add dispersant |
