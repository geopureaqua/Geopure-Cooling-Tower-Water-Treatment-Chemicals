# Geopure® 3013-D / 3013-DS: General Cooling-Water Dispersant (Softened / High-TDS Makeup)

> **Naming note:** issued under a placeholder "3013-D" / "3013-DS" designation because "Geopure 3013" is already assigned to a different, corrosion-inhibitor product (Scale & Corrosion Inhibitor for softened/low-hardness makeup, built earlier this session). This submission is a pure dispersant with no corrosion-inhibiting chemistry — a materially different product that should not share a code with the existing 3013. Recommend a distinct final product code before release.

## Product Overview

Pure dispersant/crystal-growth-modifier for open recirculating systems on **softened or high-TDS makeup water**, where elevated background ionic strength (even after softening removes hardness) can screen the electrostatic dispersancy of standard carboxylate polymers. Two threshold phosphonates (PBTC, HEDP) support scale-threshold action; polyacrylic acid and a sulfonated AMPS copolymer carry the primary dispersancy load, with the AMPS copolymer specifically chosen for its resistance to charge-screening effects in high-TDS water that would otherwise reduce a plain PAA polymer's effectiveness.

**This product contains no corrosion-inhibiting chemistry — no zinc, no molybdate, no azole/imidazole for copper.** The stated metallurgy (SS304/316, Cu, MS, GI) describes what this product is **compatible with** (won't attack or interfere with), not what it **protects**. This is designed as a dispersant companion to be co-dosed alongside a separate corrosion-inhibitor product from this line (e.g., Geopure 2000/3000/3004/2003 CH, selected to match the system's actual metallurgy and makeup water) — it is not a standalone treatment program, and should never be positioned as complete corrosion protection on its own.

Two strength variants:
- **Geopure 3013-D** (base): balanced phosphonate/dispersant loading
- **Geopure 3013-DS** ("Stronger" high-TDS variant): reduced phosphonate, increased AMPS copolymer — for makeup water where TDS is high enough that dispersancy, not scale threshold, is the dominant challenge

## Inhibition Mechanism

*(Renamed conceptually from "corrosion inhibition mechanism" to reflect what this product actually does — dispersancy and scale threshold, not corrosion protection.)*

- **PBTC**: threshold inhibition with chlorine/oxidizer stability, holding up under an oxidizing biocide program
- **HEDP**: supplemental classical CaCO₃/CaSO₄ threshold action
- **PAA**: primary electrostatic dispersant — adsorbs onto forming crystal nuclei and disrupts orderly crystal growth, keeping incipient scale particles small and suspended
- **AMPS copolymer**: sulfonate groups retain charge and dispersancy function in high-ionic-strength water where PAA's carboxylate groups can be screened by the elevated background TDS — this is the specific reason this product exists as distinct from a plain PAA-only dispersant
- **Sodium gluconate**: auxiliary metal-ion complexation, secondary role here relative to its prominence in the corrosion-inhibitor products in this line

## Dosage Guidelines

### Calculation Basis
```
Product dose in makeup (mg/L) = Target recirculating concentration / (CoC × product active fraction)
```
Since this product carries no zinc/molybdate residual to track, dose to **total dispersant residual** and/or **total phosphonate residual**, whichever your deposit-control monitoring indicates is the limiting factor for the specific system. This product's dose should be planned **alongside**, not instead of, the paired corrosion inhibitor's own dosing calculation — the two products are not substitutes for each other.

### Dosing by Makeup Water Condition (Maximum Level)

*Reinterpreted from "mainly to inhibit corrosion" — this product doesn't inhibit corrosion, so the maximum-level logic here is about dispersancy/scale-threshold capacity relative to makeup TDS, not corrosion protection:*

| Makeup TDS | Recommended Variant | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Moderate (up to ~1,000 ppm) | 3013-D | Standard phosphonate/dispersant balance sufficient |
| High (>1,000 ppm) | 3013-DS | Higher AMPS copolymer loading maintains dispersancy where PAA-dominant chemistry would lose effectiveness to charge screening |

### Dosing Point
- Co-dosed at the makeup water line or cooling tower basin alongside the paired corrosion-inhibitor product, via a separate metering pump stream (do not pre-blend concentrates unless compatibility between this product and the specific corrosion inhibitor has been confirmed)

## Application Methodology — Startup (New System)

1. Fill system with makeup water
2. Begin circulation
3. Slug dose this dispersant at elevated rate during the first 24–48 hours **specifically to help disperse construction debris, mill scale, and welding residue** common during system commissioning — this is a genuinely useful startup role distinct from the paired corrosion inhibitor's own passivation function
4. Reduce to maintenance dose per Calculation Basis
5. Ensure the paired corrosion-inhibitor product's own startup/passivation procedure is followed in parallel — this product does not substitute for it

## Passivation Protocol

*This heading doesn't apply to this product in the conventional sense — passivation describes forming a protective corrosion-inhibiting film (zinc/phosphate, molybdate, azole), and this product contains none of that chemistry. Passivation is the responsibility of the paired corrosion-inhibitor product. This dispersant's only startup-relevant role is the debris/mill-scale dispersancy described above, which runs in parallel with, not instead of, the corrosion inhibitor's passivation protocol.*

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|
| Corrosion observed despite adequate dispersant residual | This product has no corrosion-inhibiting function — check the paired corrosion-inhibitor product's own residuals instead | Do not attempt to fix a corrosion issue by adjusting this product's dose |
| Reduced dispersancy performance at high TDS on the standard 3013-D | PAA's carboxylate dispersancy is being charge-screened by elevated background ionic strength | Switch to 3013-DS for the higher AMPS copolymer loading |
| Scale/deposit at commissioning despite this product being dosed | Startup role (debris/mill-scale dispersancy) doesn't replace the corrosion inhibitor's own passivation step | Confirm the paired product's passivation protocol was actually followed |
| Confusion with the other "Geopure 3013" product | Naming collision — see note at top of this document | Use distinct product codes once assigned; do not rely on "3013" alone to identify which product is in service |
