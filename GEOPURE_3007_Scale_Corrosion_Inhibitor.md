# Geopure® 3007: Scale & Corrosion Inhibitor for Open Circulation Systems on Recycled STP Makeup (No RO/UF)

## Product Overview

High-loading, multi-mechanistic scale and corrosion inhibitor purpose-built for open recirculating systems where makeup is **recycled STP (sewage treatment plant) water without RO or UF polishing** — a materially harder makeup water quality than fresh water, RO permeate, or DM water. Unpolished secondary/tertiary-treated sewage effluent typically carries residual organic matter, elevated and variable nutrient content (including background phosphate), higher suspended solids, and a substantially higher biological load than any other makeup source addressed in this product line.

**This changes the design problem in two specific ways worth naming directly:**

1. **Background phosphate in the makeup water competes with, rather than complements, an added zinc-phosphate inhibitor.** STP effluent commonly carries residual phosphate before it ever reaches this system — adding more via the zinc-phosphate solution stacks onto that baseline, raising both calcium-phosphate scaling risk and biofouling nutrient load. PAPEMP's inclusion is a deliberate, well-suited counter to the calcium-phosphate risk specifically. **Get actual STP-effluent phosphate data before finalizing the zinc-phosphate loading** — this is a case where more phosphate is not automatically better protection.
2. **Biological control is the dominant operational risk for this system, and this formulation does not address it.** A separate, robust biocide program is essential given the elevated organic/microbial loading already present in the makeup — more so than for any other system in this product line. This formulation's dispersant package keeps particulates suspended for blowdown removal but does nothing to control biological growth directly.

**Concentrate pH vs. system operating pH:** the stated 2.5–3.5 is this concentrate's own manufacturing pH (for zinc solubility and self-preservation, same logic as Geopure 2000/3000) — **not** the recirculating system's target operating pH, which is a separate parameter not yet specified and needed for the control matrix below.

Target performance: corrosion rate <3 mpy (mild steel), calcium-phosphate and calcium-carbonate scale control under a makeup water profile with meaningfully higher and more variable fouling potential than fresh-water systems.

## Inhibition Mechanism

- **PAPEMP**: broad-spectrum scale threshold inhibition with particular strength against calcium-phosphate scale — directly counters the elevated phosphate risk this makeup water brings
- **Polymaleic acid**: calcium carbonate threshold/dispersancy
- **Polyacrylic acid**: general particulate and metal-oxide dispersancy
- **Terpolymer (Maxinal 5400)**: carboxylate/sulfonate/nonionic multifunctional deposit control, zinc-compatible
- **AMPS copolymer (Maxinal 4200)**: supplementary dispersancy under high-TDS/variable-chemistry conditions typical of STP-derived makeup
- **Zn²⁺** (from the zinc-phosphate stock): cathodic inhibition at mild steel surfaces
- **Sodium gluconate**: metal-ion complexation and general corrosion synergy

**What this formulation does not do:** control biological growth. Given this system's makeup water carries a meaningfully higher organic/microbial load than any other system in this product line, biocide selection and dosing discipline matter more here than the inhibitor chemistry itself for overall system reliability.

## Dosage Guidelines

### Calculation Basis

**Target system operating pH (separate from this concentrate's own pH) is not yet specified — recommend establishing this first**, likely in the 6.5–8.5 range depending on the balance between scale control and mild-steel corrosion protection your STP-makeup water chemistry actually requires. Everything below is provisional pending that number.

```
Product dose in makeup (mg/L) = Target recirculating concentration / (CoC × product active fraction)

Example (illustrative — zinc as the constraining parameter, same logic as Geopure 2003 CH/3004):
Target Zn = 1.5 mg/L, CoC = 5, product Zn fraction = 0.00064
Makeup dose = 1.5 / (5 × 0.00064) = 469 mg/L product in makeup
```

Note this dose is driven by the very dilute zinc fraction in this formulation (same zinc-phosphate stock used at the same 4% loading as Geopure 2003 CH/3004) — at the polymer/phosphonate concentrations in this product, the total dispersant residual delivered at this dose will likely exceed typical minimum thresholds well before the zinc target is reached, meaning **dose to the scale-control/dispersant residual, not to zinc**, is probably the more meaningful control basis here. Confirm against your own coupon and deposit-monitoring data rather than assuming zinc is the limiting parameter as it was in the other two products.

### Dosing by Makeup Hardness (Maximum Level — corrosion-limited, not scale-limited)

*Given the makeup source, hardness alone is an incomplete picture — STP-recycled water's full chemistry (residual phosphate, ammonia, chloride, TDS, and organic load) should inform the CoC ceiling more than hardness does on its own. The table below is a starting point, not a substitute for a full makeup-water analysis:*

| Makeup Water Quality | Typical Hardness (mg/L CaCO₃) | Other Parameters to Check Before Setting CoC | Maximum CoC (provisional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recycled STP (no RO/UF) | Variable — confirm actual | Residual PO₄, NH₃/NH₄⁺, chloride, TDS, BOD/COD | 3–5 *(conservative starting point given the makeup complexity — confirm against actual water analysis and coupon data before committing to a higher CoC)* |

### Dosing Point
- Makeup water line or cooling tower basin
- Continuous dosing via metering pump
- Conductivity-based blowdown control — but confirm conductivity is actually tracking CoC reliably given STP-water's more complex and variable ionic background, rather than assuming a clean conductivity/CoC relationship as in a fresh-water system

## Application Methodology

### Startup (New System)
1. Fill system with available makeup water
2. Begin circulation
3. Slug dose at 3x normal rate for first 24–48 hours (passivation)
4. Reduce to maintenance dose per the calculation basis above
5. Establish a robust biocide program from day one — do not treat this as a lower priority than in fresh-water systems, given the elevated organic/microbial loading already present in STP-derived makeup

### Passivation Protocol
- Zinc: elevated to 4–6 mg/L for 72 hours (consistent with prior products in this line, though confirm given this product's lower zinc loading may make this target harder to reach economically — see Dosage Guidelines note on the dose-limiting parameter)
- Dispersant/polymer residual: elevated during initial passivation to help manage any startup deposit load, particularly if the system has prior scale/fouling history
- pH: system operating pH, once established, held stable during this window
- After passivation, reduce to maintenance levels

## Safety Data Sheet (Summary)

### GHS Classification
- H314: Causes severe skin burns and eye damage *(consistent with the acidic pH range; confirm against actual finished-product testing)*
- H411: Toxic to aquatic life with long lasting effects (zinc)

### Environmental Note
Zinc discharge limits apply — India CPCB: Zn <5 mg/L in treated effluent. This product's phosphate contribution is modest (~0.35–0.55%), but **combined with STP makeup's own background phosphate**, total system phosphate discharge should be assessed as a whole, not just this product's contribution in isolation — get the makeup water's actual phosphate baseline before making any discharge-compliance claim.

### PPE
- Acid-resistant gloves, splash goggles, face shield for bulk handling given the acidic pH range

## Storage and Handling

- Temperature: 5–40°C
- Keep sealed to prevent evaporation and concentration increase

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|
| Calcium-phosphate scale despite adequate PAPEMP residual | STP makeup's background phosphate is higher than assumed when the program was sized | Get an actual makeup-water phosphate analysis; the zinc-phosphate solution's contribution may need reducing rather than the PAPEMP dose increasing |
| Biological fouling / biofilm despite adequate corrosion-inhibitor residual | Biocide program under-scaled for this makeup water's elevated baseline organic/microbial load — this formulation does not control biology | Review biocide selection and dosing frequency specifically for STP-derived makeup, not a fresh-water default program |
| High corrosion rate on mild steel | Low zinc/dispersant residual, or system pH not yet established/controlled | Confirm system operating pH target has actually been set (see Dosage Guidelines) and is being held; check residuals |
| Conductivity/CoC tracking looks inconsistent with actual system behavior | STP makeup's variable and complex ionic background makes conductivity a less reliable CoC proxy than in fresh-water systems | Cross-check against periodic full water analysis rather than relying on conductivity alone |
| Program cost higher than expected | Combined five-polymer/phosphonate loading is the highest in this product line | Confirm all five ingredients are genuinely each solving a distinct problem for this makeup water (see Product Overview note) rather than assuming full redundancy is required by default |
