# Geopure® 5100: Scale & Corrosion Inhibitor for Open Circulation Cooling Tower Systems

## Product Overview

Phosphonate/polymer scale and corrosion inhibitor for general open recirculating cooling tower systems, combining PAPEMP and HEDP threshold chemistry with polyacrylic acid dispersancy, BTA for copper protection, and gluconate synergy. No specific makeup water chemistry was provided with this submission — the composition, QC specs, and dosing guidance below are built generically, consistent with the approach used for the original Geopure 2000/3000 workhorse products earlier in this line, rather than the site-specific LSI-driven design used for Geopure 3008/3013.

**Design gap worth naming directly:** this formulation contains no zinc or molybdate — the primary anodic/cathodic corrosion-protection mechanisms used throughout the rest of this product line. Mild-steel protection here relies on the phosphonates' incidental anodic contribution and gluconate's general corrosion-support role, which is real but secondary. Confirm whether this is an intentional simpler/lower-cost design (or a companion product meant to pair with a supplemental corrosion inhibitor) before positioning this as a complete standalone program for higher-corrosivity systems.

Target performance: CaCO₃/CaSO₄ scale control across a broad hardness range; copper protection via BTA; mild-steel corrosion performance should be verified by coupon data given the absence of a dedicated primary inhibitor.

## Inhibition Mechanism

- **PAPEMP**: broad-spectrum threshold scale inhibition and dispersancy — the highest single-ingredient loading in this formulation, carrying most of the scale-control burden
- **HEDP**: classical CaCO₃/CaSO₄ threshold inhibition, with a secondary anodic film-forming contribution to mild-steel protection
- **Polyacrylic acid**: general particulate and metal-oxide dispersancy
- **BTA**: chemisorbed Cu(I)-BTA film formation, protecting copper/brass surfaces
- **Sodium gluconate**: metal-ion complexation and general corrosion synergy — carries proportionally more of the mild-steel protection burden here than in the zinc/molybdate-containing products elsewhere in this line, given the absence of a dedicated primary inhibitor

## Dosage Guidelines

### Calculation Basis
```
Product dose in makeup (mg/L) = Target recirculating concentration / (CoC × product active fraction)
```
No specific water chemistry was provided with this submission — dose to phosphonate/dispersant residual and BTA residual, verified against coupon data, following the general-purpose approach used for the original Geopure 2000/3000 products rather than a site-specific LSI calculation. If a specific makeup water analysis becomes available, the same CoC-projection method used for Geopure 3008/3013 can be applied here to properly size the program.

### Dosing by Makeup Hardness (Maximum Level — mainly to inhibit corrosion, not scale deposit)

*Given the absence of a dedicated primary corrosion inhibitor, treat the "corrosion, not scale" framing of this heading with particular attention here — coupon-verified mild-steel performance should carry more weight in setting the actual operating dose than the scale-driven table above.*

### Dosing Point
- Makeup water line or cooling tower basin, continuous dosing via metering pump
- Conductivity-based blowdown control to maintain CoC

## Application Methodology — Startup (New System)

1. Fill system with fresh water
2. Begin circulation
3. Slug dose at 3x normal rate for the first 24 hours
4. Reduce to maintenance dose
5. Begin biocide program after 24 hours
6. Given the absence of a dedicated primary corrosion inhibitor, prioritize an early corrosion coupon pull (30 days) to confirm mild-steel performance is adequate before committing to a full site rollout

## Passivation Protocol

- Total phosphonate: elevated to 30–40 mg/L for 72 hours
- pH: hold system operating pH in a stable, moderate range (not yet specified — recommend establishing this alongside actual site water data)
- After passivation, reduce to maintenance levels
- **Given no zinc/molybdate film-forming mechanism, passivation film quality here depends more heavily on phosphonate/gluconate chemistry alone** — verify with coupon data rather than assuming passivation performance comparable to the zinc/molybdate products in this line

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|
| High corrosion rate on mild steel | This formulation's phosphonate/gluconate-only protection may be insufficient for the system's actual corrosivity | Consider supplementing with a molybdate or zinc-containing product from this line rather than simply increasing this product's dose |
| Copper corrosion (blue water) | BTA underdosed | Verify BTA residual, increase dose |
| Scale despite adequate phosphonate residual | CoC has exceeded what the general-purpose dosing table (no site-specific water data) accounts for | Obtain an actual makeup water analysis and apply the CoC-projection method used elsewhere in this product line |
| Recurring BTA-quantity errors in submitted formulations | This is the third instance across this product line's formulations (Geopure 2003 CH twice, now 5100) | Worth checking whether the source recipe/template being used to draft these formulations has BTA specified incorrectly at the source, rather than treating each instance as an independent error |
