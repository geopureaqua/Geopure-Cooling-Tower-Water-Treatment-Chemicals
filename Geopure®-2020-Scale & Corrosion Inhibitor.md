# Geopure®-2020: Scale & Corrosion Inhibitor

## Product Overview

Blend of phosphonates and polymeric dispersants for calcium carbonate and calcium sulfate scale prevention in low to medium pressure boilers (up to 30 kg/cm2). Operates via threshold inhibition and crystal modification mechanisms.

Not suitable for systems requiring zero phosphorus discharge or boilers >40 kg/cm2.

## Manufacturing Procedure

### Batch Size: 1000 kg

| Step | Action | Duration | Notes |
|------|--------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Charge 450 kg DM water | - | Ambient temperature |
| 2 | Start agitator at 100 RPM | - | - |
| 3 | Add 100 kg HEDP (60% active solution) | 10 min | Slowly, pH will drop to ~1.5 |
| 4 | Add 80 kg ATMP (50% active solution) | 10 min | pH remains acidic |
| 5 | Slowly add 100 kg NaOH (50% solution) | 20 min | CAUTION: exothermic. Target pH 5.0-6.0. Do not overshoot |
| 6 | Mix for 10 min | 10 min | Allow temperature to stabilize |
| 7 | Add 65 kg PAA (40% active solution) | 10 min | - |
| 8 | Add 30 kg sodium gluconate (solid) | 15 min | Dissolve fully before proceeding |
| 9 | Makeup to 1000 kg with DM water | 5 min | - |
| 10 | Adjust pH to 5.5-6.5 with NaOH if needed | 5-10 min | - |
| 11 | Mix 15 min, sample for QC | 15 min | - |

Total production time: ~2 hours

## Dosage Guidelines

### Calculation Basis
Threshold inhibition of CaCO3 requires 2-5 mg/L HEDP active in boiler water. Dosing accounts for cycles of concentration (CoC).

```
Dose (mg/L in feedwater) = Target residual in boiler / CoC

Where CoC = Boiler water TDS / Feedwater TDS

Example:
Target HEDP residual: 5 mg/L in boiler, CoC = 10
Required in feedwater = 5/10 = 0.5 mg/L HEDP active
Product dose = 0.5 / 0.10 (HEDP fraction) = 5 mg/L product in feedwater
```

### Typical Dosing Ranges

| Feedwater Hardness (mg/L CaCO3) | Product Dose (mg/L in feedwater) |
|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| <50 | 5-10 |
| 50-150 | 10-25 |
| 150-300 | 25-50 |
| >300 | 50-80 (consider softening feedwater) |

### Dosing Point
- Feedwater tank or feedwater line upstream of economizer
- Continuous dosing preferred, slug dosing acceptable for small boilers

## Application Methodology

### Startup
1. Determine feedwater hardness, alkalinity, and silica
2. Calculate CoC target based on blowdown rate
3. Set initial dose at mid-range
4. Monitor phosphonate residual in boiler water after 24 hours
5. Adjust to maintain 3-8 mg/L PO4 residual

### Monitoring Schedule

| Parameter | Frequency | Target |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Phosphonate residual (boiler) | Daily | 3-8 mg/L as PO4 |
| Hardness (boiler water) | Daily | ND (non-detectable) |
| Iron (feedwater) | Weekly | <0.5 mg/L |
| Blowdown rate | Continuous | Per TDS target |

## Storage and Handling

- HDPE containers only. Do not use mild steel or galvanized containers (acidic pH)
- Temperature: 5-45°C
- Avoid contact with strong alkalis (may precipitate calcium phosphonate if contaminated with hard water)
- Shelf life: 12 months

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Scale on tubes despite dosing | Underdosing, high hardness excursion | Increase dose, check softener or RO output |
| Phosphonate residual undetectable | High blowdown consuming chemical, thermal degradation (>250°C) | Reduce blowdown if TDS allows, check boiler pressure |
| Iron deposits | Feedwater iron >1 mg/L overwhelming gluconate chelation | Add pre-treatment iron removal, increase gluconate fraction |
| Foaming | Over-dosing polymer component, organic ingress | Reduce dose, check condensate contamination, increase surface blow |
| Sludge accumulation | Insufficient dispersant, infrequent bottom blow | Increase bottom blowdown frequency, review polymer level |
