# Geopure® 5200: General Dispersant for Open Circulation Cooling Tower Systems

## Product Overview

Three-component polymeric dispersant for open recirculating cooling tower systems — AMPS copolymer, polyacrylic acid, and polymaleic acid, with no phosphonate chemistry and no corrosion-inhibiting components. Provides particulate dispersancy and moderate polymeric scale-threshold action, but **is not a substitute for a dedicated phosphonate threshold inhibitor** on aggressive, high-LSI scale-forming water — pair with a phosphonate-containing product (e.g., Geopure 3013-D, or the appropriate full corrosion-inhibitor product from this line) where strong CaCO₃ threshold protection is required.

**No corrosion-inhibiting chemistry is present** — no zinc, molybdate, phosphonate, or azole/BTA. This product must be paired with a separate corrosion inhibitor selected for the system's actual metallurgy; it does not provide standalone corrosion protection under any circumstances.

## Inhibition Mechanism

*(This product disperses and moderately inhibits scale-forming particulate; it does not inhibit corrosion. Reframed accordingly.)*

- **AMPS copolymer**: sulfonate-group dispersancy, resistant to charge-screening in high-TDS water
- **Polyacrylic acid**: electrostatic dispersancy, adsorbs onto forming crystal nuclei
- **Polymaleic acid**: calcium carbonate threshold action and dispersancy — the closest thing to dedicated scale-threshold chemistry in this formulation, though generally regarded as less potent against CaCO₃ specifically than a phosphonate inhibitor like HEDP or PBTC

**What this product does not do:** provide any corrosion protection, or strong CaCO₃ threshold inhibition comparable to a phosphonate-containing product. Both require pairing with another product from this line.

## Dosage Guidelines

### Calculation Basis
```
Product dose in makeup (mg/L) = Target recirculating concentration / (CoC × product active fraction)
```
Dose to total polymer/dispersant residual. This product's dose should be planned **in addition to**, not instead of, a paired phosphonate-containing scale product and a separate corrosion inhibitor — it covers neither function on its own.

### Dosing by Makeup Hardness (Maximum Level — reframed, since this product does not inhibit corrosion)

### Dosing Point
- Co-dosed at the makeup water line or cooling tower basin, via a separate metering stream from both the phosphonate scale product and the corrosion inhibitor it's paired with

## Application Methodology — Startup (New System)

1. Fill system with fresh water
2. Begin circulation
3. Slug dose during first 24–48 hours to help disperse construction debris and mill scale
4. Reduce to maintenance dose per Calculation Basis
5. Ensure the paired phosphonate scale product and corrosion inhibitor are dosed per their own startup procedures — this product does not substitute for either

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|
| Corrosion observed | This product provides no corrosion protection | Check the paired corrosion-inhibitor product's residuals — do not adjust this product's dose in response |
| CaCO₃ scale despite adequate dispersant residual | This product's threshold capability is polymeric, not phosphonate-grade | Confirm a phosphonate-containing scale product is being co-dosed per design; this product alone is not sized for aggressive scale-forming water |
| Measured pH significantly outside expected 2–4 range | Raw material supply may differ from the assumed part-neutralized commercial grades | Confirm actual supplier specifications for all three polymers before adjusting the manufacturing procedure |
