# Geopure®-7000: Scale Inhibitor / Antifoulant (Sulfonated Copolymer)

## Product Overview

High-performance polymeric Scale Inhibitor for control of Scale, suspended solids, silt, iron oxide, and process contaminants in cooling water. Maintains heat transfer efficiency by preventing deposition on condenser and heat exchanger surfaces.

## Chemical Composition

| Component | CAS Number | % w/w | Functional Role |
|-----------|-----------|-------|-----------------|
| AA/AMPS/SS terpolymer (Acrylic acid/2-Acrylamido-2-methylpropane sulfonic acid/Sulfonated styrene) (30% active) | Proprietary | 30.0-40.0 | High-temperature stable dispersant, sulfonated backbone resists Ca2+ bridging |
| Polyacrylic acid (MW 5000, 40% active) | 9003-01-4 | 10.0-15.0 | General dispersant, crystal modifier |
| Sodium hydroxide (50%) | 1310-73-2 | 2.0-4.0 | pH adjustment |
| Demineralized water | 7732-18-5 | Balance | Solvent |

## Manufacturing Procedure

### Batch Size: 1000 kg

| Step | Action | Duration | Notes |
|------|--------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Charge 500 kg DM water | - | Ambient |
| 2 | Start agitator 100 RPM | - | - |
| 3 | Add 350 kg AA/AMPS/SS terpolymer (30% active) | 15 min | Viscous, pour slowly |
| 4 | Add 120 kg PAA (40% active) | 10 min | - |
| 5 | Adjust pH to 6.0-7.0 with NaOH (50%), ~30 kg | 10 min | - |
| 6 | Makeup to 1000 kg | 5 min | - |
| 7 | Mix 15 min, QC | 15 min | - |

## Dosage Guidelines

| Water Quality | Product Dose in Makeup (mg/L) |
|-------------|------------------------------|
| Clean makeup, low TSS | 10-20 |
| Moderate dust/airborne contamination | 20-40 |
| High silt, process contamination | 40-80 |
| Side-stream filter present | Reduce dose by 30-40% |

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Fouling deposits despite dosing | Polymer insufficient for contaminant load | Increase dose, add sidestream filtration |
| Foaming | Overdosing | Reduce dose, check for surfactant ingress |
| Muddy basin water | Normal at high TSS, check blowdown | Increase blowdown rate, verify sidestream filter |
