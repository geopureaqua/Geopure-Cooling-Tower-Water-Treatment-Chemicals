# Geopure®-3100 BR & Geopure®-3100 BRA: Stabilized Bromine Oxidizing Biocide System

## System Overview

Geopure®-3100 BR and Geopure®-3100 BRA together form a stabilized bromine biocide system for microbiological control in cooling towers and open recirculating systems. The two products are supplied, priced, and shipped **separately** and are combined only in dilute form at the point of dosing into the cooling water system.

- **Geopure®-3100 BR** — Sodium bromide solution (bromine source)
- **Geopure®-3100 BRA** — Bromide activator (sodium hypochlorite–based oxidant)

Stabilized bromine is effective across pH 6.0–9.0, unlike chlorine alone, which loses efficacy above pH 7.5. The active biocidal species is hypobromous acid (HOBr), pKa 8.7. At pH 8.5, ~60% of available halogen remains as HOBr, versus only ~15% as HOCl from chlorine alone — this is the core technical justification for specifying the Geopure®-3100 BR/BRA system over straight chlorination in high-pH or high-COC cooling systems.

### Activation Chemistry (occurs in dilute form in the cooling water / mixing chamber)

```
NaBr + NaOCl → NaOBr + NaCl
Sodium bromide + Sodium hypochlorite → Sodium hypobromite + Sodium chloride
```

Dosing ratio: 1 part Geopure®-3100 BR : 1.2 parts Geopure®-3100 BRA (by volume)

**IMPORTANT — Never pre-mix Geopure®-3100 BR and Geopure®-3100 BRA in concentrated form at the drum/storage stage.** Mixing occurs only after both products enter the dilute cooling water stream, via separate metering pumps and separate injection points (or a static mixer manifold immediately upstream of the tower basin/sump return line).

# Product 2: Geopure®-3100 BRA (Bromide Activator)

## Product Overview

Geopure®-3100 BRA is the oxidant/activator component of the Geopure stabilized bromine system. It is a stabilized sodium hypochlorite solution formulated specifically for on-site activation of Geopure®-3100 BR. Geopure®-3100 BRA should not be used as a standalone biocide — its role is exclusively to activate the bromide ion supplied by Geopure®-3100 BR into hypobromous acid.

## Chemical Composition

# System Dosage Guidelines (Geopure®-3100 BR + Geopure®-3100 BRA)

## Target Residual
0.3–0.5 mg/L free residual bromine (as Cl2 equivalent) continuous; 1–2 mg/L for shock treatment.

```
Geopure-3100 BR dose (mg/L) = Target Br2 residual × Demand factor / NaBr active fraction

Demand factor: 3–5x for typical cooling water (accounts for organic demand)

Example:
Target: 0.5 mg/L residual, Demand factor: 4, NaBr fraction: 0.40
Geopure-3100 BR dose  = 0.5 × 4 / 0.40 = 5 mg/L
Geopure-3100 BRA dose = 5 × 1.2 = 6 mg/L
```

## Typical Dosing

| Condition | Geopure-3100 BR (mg/L) | Geopure-3100 BRA (mg/L) | Frequency |
|-----------|------------------------|--------------------------|-----------|
| Continuous | 3–8 | 4–10 | 24/7 via metering pump |
| Shock (weekly) | 15–25 | 18–30 | 2–4 hours |
| High bio-load | 8–15 | 10–18 | Increase until ORP >400 mV |

## Control Method
- ORP-based dosing preferred (target 350–450 mV)
- Free bromine test (DPD method) for verification
- Never dose based on timer alone — pair with ORP or conductivity-linked bleed control for accurate COC-adjusted feed

## Application Methodology

### Startup
1. Establish circulation and existing scale/corrosion treatment program
2. Begin Geopure®-3100 BR + Geopure®-3100 BRA feed simultaneously, via **separate** metering pumps to **separate** injection points
3. Adjust feed rate to achieve 0.3–0.5 mg/L free bromine residual
4. Verify with DPD test kit (reads as "total chlorine" on standard kits)
5. Set ORP controller setpoint at 380 mV, dead band ±20 mV

### Legionella Management
- Maintain free bromine >0.3 mg/L at all times
- Quarterly Legionella testing per ASHRAE 188
- Shock to 2 mg/L if Legionella detected >1000 CFU/L
- Document all dosing and test results

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| No residual despite dosing | High organic demand, biofilm | Shock dose, clean tower, increase dispersant |
| ORP erratic | Probe fouling, interference | Clean ORP probe, check for H2S interference |
| Bromine smell | Overdosing | Reduce feed rate, check controller |
| Brown discoloration | Tribromamine formation at high pH | Reduce pH to <8.5, ensure adequate Geopure-3100 BRA:BR ratio |
| Legionella positive | Inadequate continuous dosing, dead legs | Increase continuous residual, eliminate stagnant zones |
| Residual present but system still fouling | Geopure-3100 BR:BRA ratio imbalance | Re-verify 1:1.2 dosing ratio at both pumps independently; check for pump calibration drift |
