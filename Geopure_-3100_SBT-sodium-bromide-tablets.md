# Geopure®-3100 SBT: Sodium Bromide Tablets

## Product Overview

Geopure®-3100 SBT is a solid, slow-dissolving sodium bromide tablet formulation, designed as a simplified, single-product alternative to the liquid Geopure®-3100 BR / BRA two-part system for sites that prefer erosion-feeder dosing over dual metering pumps.

Geopure®-3100 SBT tablets dissolve in a side-stream erosion feeder to release a controlled, continuous concentration of bromide ion into the cooling water. This bromide is then activated to hypobromous acid (HOBr) using the site's existing chlorination system (gas chlorinator, hypochlorite dosing, or electrochlorination) — or, where no on-site chlorine source exists, in combination with Geopure®-3100 BRA fed at a reduced makeup rate. This makes Geopure®-3100 SBT particularly suited to sites that already dose chlorine/hypochlorite for disinfection but want to upgrade to bromine chemistry for its superior high-pH performance, without adding a second liquid metering system.

As with the liquid system, the active biocidal species is hypobromous acid (HOBr, pKa 8.7), effective across pH 6.0–9.0 — a significant advantage over straight chlorination above pH 7.5, which is common in high-COC cooling towers dosed with alkaline scale/corrosion inhibitor programs.

## Dosage Guidelines

### Target Residual
0.3–0.5 mg/L free residual bromine (as Cl2 equivalent) continuous; 1–2 mg/L for shock treatment — same targets as the liquid Geopure®-3100 BR/BRA system.

### Feeder Sizing
```
Tablet consumption rate (g/hr) = Target Br2 residual × System volume flow (m³/hr) × Demand factor / NaBr active fraction

Demand factor: 3–5x for typical cooling water (accounts for organic demand)

Worked example:
Target: 0.5 mg/L residual, side-stream flow: 2 m³/hr, Demand factor: 4, NaBr active fraction: 0.94
Consumption = 0.5 × 2 × 4 / 0.94 ≈ 4.3 g/hr → approx. 1 x 25 mm tablet every 2 hours, or size feeder for continuous top-up
```

### Chlorine/Activator Pairing
- If site already doses chlorine/hypochlorite: no separate activator required — maintain existing chlorine residual at 1.2x the bromide feed rate (by available halogen equivalent) to fully activate the bromide pool
- If no on-site chlorine source: dose Geopure®-3100 BRA at reduced rate (approx. 60–70% of the standard BR/BRA system rate, since SBT already supplies concentrated bromide)

### Typical Feeder Loading

| Condition | Tablet Loading | Refill Frequency (typical) |
|-----------|----------------|------------------------------|
| Continuous | Feeder 50–75% full | Weekly, site-dependent on tower volume |
| Shock (weekly) | Top off feeder + increase side-stream flow rate | As needed |
| High bio-load | Increase side-stream flow through feeder | Until ORP >400 mV |

### Control Method
- ORP-based verification preferred (target 350–450 mV)
- Free bromine test (DPD method) for verification
- Visually inspect feeder monthly for tablet channeling (uneven dissolution creating a bypass path) — repack or replace feeder bed if observed

## Application Methodology

### Startup
1. Install side-stream erosion feeder on a bleed line off the recirculating pump discharge
2. Load feeder with Geopure®-3100 SBT tablets per sizing calculation
3. Confirm existing chlorine/hypochlorite dosing is active, or begin reduced-rate Geopure®-3100 BRA dosing
4. Adjust side-stream flow through the feeder to achieve 0.3–0.5 mg/L free bromine residual at the tower
5. Verify with DPD test kit (reads as "total chlorine" on standard kits)
6. Set ORP controller setpoint at 380 mV, dead band ±20 mV

### Legionella Management
- Maintain free bromine >0.3 mg/L at all times
- Quarterly Legionella testing per ASHRAE 188
- Shock to 2 mg/L if Legionella detected >1000 CFU/L (increase side-stream flow and/or activator dose)
- Document all dosing and test results

## Safety Data Sheet (Summary)

### GHS Classification
- H302: Harmful if swallowed
- H319: Causes serious eye irritation
- Dust may cause respiratory irritation during tablet handling/loading — use dust mask when loading feeders in poorly ventilated areas

### Handling
- No oxidizing hazard as a standalone solid — safer to transport, store, and handle on-site than liquid hypochlorite-based activators
- Keep sealed and dry — tablets are hygroscopic and will degrade/cake if exposed to humidity
- Avoid mixing tablet dust with chlorine-based products in concentrated/dry form

## Storage and Handling

- Store in original sealed HDPE drums or moisture-barrier bags
- Cool, dry storage, <40°C, away from direct sunlight
- Shelf life: 36 months from date of manufacture if kept sealed
- Do not store in the same enclosed space as concentrated oxidizers (e.g., Geopure®-3100 BRA, calcium hypochlorite) — separate by secondary containment or distance

## Troubleshooting Guide

| Problem | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| No residual despite adequate tablet loading | Feeder channeling, low side-stream flow | Repack feeder bed, increase side-stream flow, check for tablet bridging |
| Tablets dissolving too fast | Excess side-stream flow, undersized feeder for tower demand | Throttle side-stream flow, verify feeder sizing against calculation |
| Tablets caking / not dissolving evenly | Moisture ingress during storage | Discard caked stock, improve storage sealing, rotate stock FIFO |
| ORP erratic | Probe fouling, interference | Clean ORP probe, check for H2S interference |
| Brown discoloration | Tribromamine formation at high pH / activator imbalance | Reduce pH to <8.5, re-check chlorine/activator pairing ratio |
| Legionella positive | Inadequate continuous dosing, dead legs | Increase side-stream flow / activator dose, eliminate stagnant zones |
